A Bio-Adaptive Metronome

The metronome is an essential tool used by every music therapist. We can help people keep better time by making a metronome that listens.

01 It listens

Therapists rely on a metronome for a steady beat, but they use it as a reference and do most of the work by ear, adjusting the tempo to the patient in real time. Our hardware closes the gap between an outdated tool and what the work actually needs.

02 It measures

Using Merry's hardware, it senses the patient in real time: heart rate, step rate, speech cadence, and EEG-derived state. That is the read a therapist makes by feel, now captured as signal (RTD).

03 It defines

The therapist's judgment becomes an objective, repeatable measure. The results come back clear and defined, with our hardware right in the middle of it.

how we can help

Music therapy is growing across many markets, and we have a rare chance to help shape it early. Here is where we come in.

Without measurable results there is no billing code, no clear provider status, and the field leans on philanthropy to survive. Our hardware gives the field its first objective, per-session measure, and measurement is what a billing code is built on. That is the path from unpaid work to a reimbursable service, and a market that does not exist yet.

97530. Today a therapist bills under a borrowed code, a generic "therapeutic activities" line, because music therapy has none of its own. A defined, measurable service is how the field earns a code that fits, and an earned code is a direct line to reimbursement. The absence is the opportunity.
