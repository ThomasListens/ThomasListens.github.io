iii. the build07 / 14

Your perspective makes a difference.

Alpha frequency shapes the precision of how we sample the world.

— Romei & Tarasi (2026)

IAF reflects the rate at which the brain samples sensory and temporal information.

Tempo = HR × m × μ

HR : 80 BPM m : 2.00 walk IAF : 8.0 Hz μ : 1.25 Tempo : 200 BPM

μ = IAF₀ ⁄ IAF — your alpha is the pivot. Sample faster and μ dips below 1, easing the tempo; slower lifts it.
